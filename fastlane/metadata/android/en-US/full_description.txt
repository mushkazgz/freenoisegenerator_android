Free Noise Generator is a minimal Android app for playing deep brown noise when you want a steady, calm sound in the background.

The app generates the sound directly on your device, so it does not need bundled audio files or an internet connection. Open it, press Start noise and adjust the volume, bass, low mids and timer to match your space.

Designed for quiet use at night or while focusing, Free Noise Generator includes background playback, notification controls and a low-light idle screensaver with a warm ember glow and a soft night sky.

Features:

- Procedural deep brown noise.
- Simple one-screen interface.
- Volume, bass and low mids controls.
- Timer up to 12 hours.
- Background playback with notification controls.
- Optional low-light idle screensaver.
- Bluetooth settings shortcut.
- No ads, no accounts and no analytics.
