# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

Free Noise Generator is a simple Android app for playing procedurally generated deep brown noise.

## Data Collection

Free Noise Generator does not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

The app does not require an account, does not include advertising, does not use analytics SDKs and does not send usage data to a server controlled by the developer.

## Local App Data

The app stores a small amount of preference data locally on the device, such as volume, bass, low mids, timer and screensaver settings. This data stays on the user's device and is used only to restore the app's settings.

## Permissions

The app may request notification permission on supported Android versions so it can show playback controls while audio is running in the background.

The app uses Android foreground service permissions for media playback so the generated noise can continue playing when the app is minimized.

## Bluetooth

The app includes a shortcut to Android's Bluetooth settings. Bluetooth pairing and connection are handled by Android, not by Free Noise Generator.

## Donations

The app may include an optional PayPal donation link. Opening that link leaves the app and is handled by PayPal under PayPal's own terms and privacy policy.
