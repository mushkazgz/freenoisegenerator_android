# Google Play Data Safety Draft

This document is a draft to help fill in the Google Play Console Data safety form. Confirm the final answers before submitting.

## Data Collection

Suggested answer: The app does not collect or share user data.

Reasoning:

- No account system.
- No analytics SDK.
- No advertising SDK.
- No backend service.
- No personal data sent to the developer.

## Data Sharing

Suggested answer: No user data is shared by the app.

## Security Practices

Suggested answer: Data is not collected or transmitted.

## Local Preferences

The app stores only local settings on the device:

- Volume.
- Bass level.
- Low mids level.
- Timer setting.
- Screensaver preference.

These settings are not transmitted outside the device.

## Permissions Explanation

### Notifications

Used to show playback status and pause/stop controls while noise is playing in the background.

### Foreground Service / Media Playback

Used so generated noise can continue playing when the app is minimized, with a persistent notification and user-visible playback controls.

## External Links

The optional PayPal donation link opens outside the app. PayPal handles that interaction under its own privacy policy.
